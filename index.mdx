City Metro Trip Planner is the official web interface for the City Metro transit network. It lets riders explore lines and stations on an interactive canvas map, plan journeys with real-time arrival data, follow live vehicles turn-by-turn, and save favourite routes to their account — all from a mobile-first progressive web experience deployed on Cloudflare Workers.
